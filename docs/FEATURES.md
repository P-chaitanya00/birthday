### 1. Interactive Calendar Countdown Intro
- **Timeline Progression**: A dedicated welcome screen tracking dates from November 18 through the birthday on November 22.
- **Heart Checkpoint Nodes**: Completed days turn into glowing heart checkpoints with micro-animations.
- **Milestone Reveal**: Stepping into November 22 triggers a grand celebration banner and celebratory fanfare.
- **Quick Skip Option**: A subtle *"Skip to Birthday Celebration ➔"* link for instant access to the main page.

---

### 2. Floating Particle Canvas & Ambient Music
- **HTML5 Canvas Animation**: Lightweight particle engine running at 60 FPS rendering floating, drifting hearts with varied sizes, opacities, and speeds.
- **Synthesized Web Audio Engine**: Procedural ambient chimes and arpeggios crafted with Web Audio API oscillators—zero external audio files or broken streaming links.
- **Audio Control Pill**: A floating, unobtrusive toggle button allowing playback pausing and resuming anytime.
- **Sparkle Mouse Trail**: Playful floating stars and sparkles following cursor/touch movements.

---

### 3. Hero Section & Memory Frames
- **Rose-Gold Gradient Typography**: Styled in `Playfair Display` and `Cinzel` serif fonts.
- **Trio Memory Showcase**: Three portrait photo frames displaying memorable couple moments with soft rose-gold drop shadows and tilt effects.
- **Personalized Salutation**: Centered celebration headline crafted specifically for *"My Bangaru ❤️"*.

---

### 4. Deluxe 3D Patisserie Birthday Cake
- **Multi-Tier Illustrated Cake**: Layered strawberry cream and champagne frosting cake with decorative piped rosettes and chocolate swirls.
- **Interactive Flickering Candles**: Lit candles with flickering flame animations.
- **Blow-Out Interaction**: Tap individual candles or click **"Blow Out Candles"** to trigger:
  - Realistic smoke puff animations
  - Melodic chime arpeggio
  - Multi-directional confetti cannons
  - Revealed birthday surprise card beneath the cake

---

### 5. Vintage Love Letter with Glass Blur Modal
- **On-Page Envelope Cover**: Styled like a classic vintage postal envelope featuring postal stamps, wax seal with embossed heart, and cursive calligraphy address.
- **Frosted Glass Blur Modal**: Tapping the envelope smoothly opens the full heartfelt love letter (written in Telugu & English) over a deep blurred backdrop (`backdrop-filter: blur(14px)`).
- **Smooth Close & Fold**: Close button and *"Fold Letter & Return"* button for seamless navigation.

---

### 6. Interactive Reasons Card Deck
- **Card Deck Mechanism**: Interactive stack displaying reasons why she is cherished.
- **3D Card Transitions**: Tapping *"Next Reason ➔"* smoothly swishes the top card away and presents the next card with subtle 3D rotations.
- **Romantic Micro-Copy**: Thoughtfully composed romantic affirmations highlighting her smile, kindness, laughter, and presence.

---

### 7. Snapshots of Us: Filmstrip Reel Carousel
- **Filmstrip Reel Stage**: Responsive carousel showcasing 5 photographic memories.
- **Full-Screen Floating Navigation Arrows**: Left and right circular screen-edge buttons for effortless photo switching on both mobile and desktop.
- **Synchronized Pagination Dots**: Live indicator dots highlighting the active photo index.
- **Full-Screen Lightbox**: Clicking any photo opens an enlarged romantic lightbox viewer with caption overlay.

---

### 8. Realistic 3D Gift Box & Secret Note
- **Luxury 3D Gift Box Visual**:
  - Deep velvet crimson box body with multi-layer shadow depth
  - Golden satin ribbon cross with metallic sheen
  - Realistic 3D silk ribbon bow with arched loops and fishtail tails
  - Metallic hanging gift tag: *"To: Bangaru ❤️"*
  - Ambient breathing glow and floating sparkle clusters
- **Interactive Unboxing Animation**: Clicking the box lifts and tilts the lid with a festive confetti burst.
- **Pure Frosted Glass Background Blur**: The entire website behind the note card softly blurs (`backdrop-filter: blur(14px)`), keeping the celebratory atmosphere visible.
- **Medium Parchment Secret Note**: Centered card with gold filigree corner ornaments and the heartfelt handwritten message:
  > **“as soon as u see me come and give me a tight hug”**
- **Interactive Action Button**: *"🤗 I Promise A Tight Hug! ❤️"* button with fireworks confetti and toast notification.

---

### 9. Sweet Interactive Wish Wall
- **Interactive Sticky Notes Board**: A cork-board style message section with pastel sticky notes.
- **Instant Pinning**: Enter any promise, thought, or wish and click *"Pin Note 📌"*.
- **Local Persistence**: Notes are saved in browser `localStorage`, ensuring memories remain saved across page refreshes.

---

### 10. Built-in Personalizer & Settings
- **In-App Customization Modal**: Gear icon in the top header allows instant updates to:
  - Recipient Name
  - Sender / Boyfriend Name
  - Love Letter Message Content
  - Photo URLs (Photo 1 through Photo 8)
- **Zero-Code Customization**: Updates take effect immediately and persist via `localStorage` with a 1-click *"Reset to Defaults"* option.
